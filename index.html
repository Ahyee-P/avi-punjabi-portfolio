Summary

I work across the full stack of a hardware project: deriving the control law, writing the firmware and dashboards that measure it, building the rig that tests it, and running the analysis that shows the part will hold.

At Texas A&M I led robot-kinematics research for Canadarm2, ran data acquisition for a sensor-fused suspension test rig, and captained VEXU robotics teams through two international competitions. I'm now designing hardware at Neuro42 and Space Kinetic while I finish my MEng at Berkeley. I do my best work on small, fast-moving teams that run tight design–simulation–test loops, especially in robotics and autonomous systems, where controls, mechanics, and electronics all meet.
